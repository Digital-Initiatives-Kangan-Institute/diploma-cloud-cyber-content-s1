- Final-assessment placement: AT2 (the implementation build) completes immediately before the spare buffer; AT1 (the design / DR plan) sits within the teaching block
- Session rhythm: 3 × 3h sessions/week across weeks 9–18 (arrangement across teaching days settled at delivery planning); the cohort also carries CL3's 2 sessions/week in the same weeks
